\begin{abstract}
This draft captures the current experimental and empirical core of
\texttt{agentic\_flu\_model\_discovery}. The benchmark compares deterministic,
probabilistic, fractional, hospitalization-aware, delayed-observation, and
standard forecasting baselines against constrained non-LLM discovery and
same-grammar discovery ablations. The current frozen evidence supports
age-aware and objective-aware model selection under structural and observation
uncertainty rather than a single globally dominant model family.
\end{abstract}
